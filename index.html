Keybinds

Click a button, then press the key (mouse side button or scroll wheel) you want. Modifiers like Shift, Ctrl and Alt work. Esc cancels, Backspace clears. One key can fire several actions, like a macro: share it between one global-cooldown ability and any number of off-GCD ones (trinkets, potion, Bloodrage, Heroic Strike). Two GCD abilities on one key swap instead.

Settings

Rage uses the live game's conversion (about 231 at level 60). WoWSims uses ~198, so it gives roughly 15% more rage from white hits and your DPS will read lower here than in a WoWSims run.

When on, pressing Overpower swaps to Battle Stance first, and Whirlwind or Recklessness swaps to Berserker Stance, like a stance macro. Rage above your Tactical Mastery limit is lost on each swap, as in game.
